Getting your App Password

  1. Go to myaccount.google.com/security and turn on 2-Step Verification.
  2. Visit myaccount.google.com/apppasswords.
  3. Create a new App Password (name it "Gixvor Mail Fetcher") and copy the 16 characters.
  4. Make sure IMAP is enabled under Gmail → Settings → Forwarding and POP/IMAP.
Nothing is stored. Your App Password is used once to open the IMAP connection and is kept only in this browser session.
