What it does

✉

Sender-scoped search

Hand it one address and it uses Gmail's IMAP SEARCH FROM to pull only the messages that matter, newest first.

🔒

Password never stored

Your App Password lives only in the server-side session for the length of your visit — it's never written to the database.

📋

Clean, readable results

Subjects, senders, dates, and bodies are parsed out of raw MIME so you get an inbox view, not a wall of headers.

🔄

Searchable history

Every search is cached, so you can revisit results, or hit refresh to pull the latest without retyping anything.

How it works

  1. 01

    Enable IMAP & make an App Password

    Turn on IMAP access in Gmail settings, then generate a 16-character App Password (requires 2-Step Verification).

  2. 02

    Connect & tell it who to look for

    Enter your Gmail address, the App Password, and the sender you want every message from.

  3. 03

    It logs in over IMAP

    Gixvor opens a secure IMAP_SSL connection, selects your mailbox, and runs a FROM search server-side on Gmail.

  4. 04

    Messages are parsed & laid out

    Each match is decoded from raw MIME — subject, sender, date, plain-text body, attachments — and shown as a card.
